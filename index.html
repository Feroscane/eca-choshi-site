Experienced English Teachers

Eiken certification

We are a Eiken registered test center and provide extensive Eiken study courses for written and speaking tests

For anybody who wants to learn and practice!

We create customized English conversation courses for people of all ages and skill levels. Classes are for people of similar levels for up to 4-5 students.

私たちは各年齢と英語力の方々が効率よく学べる学習メニューを作成しています。各クラスは、最大4〜5人で構成されています。

More than just English!

While our main focus is English, we also have specialised tutors and courses for Mathematics, Social Studies, Science, and Japanese Subjects.

私たちの主な指導教科は英語ですが、数学、社会科、科学、および日本語科目のための専門担当講師による塾コースもあります。
